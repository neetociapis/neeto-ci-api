---

The **NeetoCI CLI** (`neetoci`) lets you manage NeetoCI from your terminal. It covers the [v2 REST API](/api/introduction): list projects, trigger and inspect CI jobs, read per-pod logs and resource metrics, manage environment variables, and open SSH debug sessions on finished jobs.

It is designed for people comfortable with a terminal or HTTP. You do not need to be a developer: run `neetoci setup` to connect an AI assistant such as Claude or Cursor, then describe the task in plain language.

## Why use the CLI?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Script your pipeline" icon="terminal">
    Trigger jobs, check their status, and rerun failures from shell scripts and other CI steps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Debug from the terminal" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Pull a job's per-pod log files and CPU and memory metrics, pipe them to `jq`, and find OOM kills without opening the browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage configuration" icon="key">
    Create, rotate, and delete global and project environment variables without leaving your shell.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Built for AI agents" icon="robot">
    Use token-efficient `--toon` output and one-command setup for Claude, Cursor, Copilot, and more.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What you need

1. Access to one or more NeetoCI workspaces.
2. Permission to manage the resources you want to work with (projects, CI jobs, environment variables, and so on).
3. The `neetoci` binary. See [Installation](/cli/installation).

<Note>
  Unlike the API, which authenticates with an `X-Api-Key` header, the CLI signs
  you in through your browser and stores credentials locally. See
  [Authentication](/cli/authentication).
</Note>
